# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### How do I access Plex?
Click the "Watch" button in the sidebar to open Plex directly.

### Why can't I find a movie/show?
We use TMDB (The Movie Database) for search. If content isn't there, it won't appear here. Try searching with the original title or release year.

### How long does it take for requests to be fulfilled?
Most requests are processed within a few hours. Older or rare content may take longer.

## Requests

### Can I cancel a request?
Yes, go to Manage > find your request > click the options menu > Cancel.

### Why was my request declined?
Requests may be declined if:
- The content isn't available in good quality
- It violates content policies
- It's a duplicate request

### How many requests can I make?
Check your profile settings to see your quota.

## Playback

### Content isn't playing properly
1. Try a different player/device
2. Check your internet connection
3. Report an issue with the specific title

### How do I watch on mobile?
Download the Plex app and sign in with your account.

## Account

### How do I change my notification preferences?
Go to Profile > Settings > Notifications.

### I forgot my password
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page, or contact an admin.
